If you need support right now

SafeSpeak is not an emergency service and reports are not monitored around the clock. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency hotline, contact campus security or the school office, or reach out to a guidance counselor, teacher, family member, or another trusted adult. Do not wait for a response here.

How SafeSpeak works

Three calm steps from speaking up to hearing back.

Simple by design
1 Submit

Share a concern or confession. Reports are anonymous by default, and you can attach images if they help explain the situation.

2 Review

The SafeSpeak team reviews the report, checks its category, and decides what action or referral is needed.

3 Respond

If you linked the report to your account, you can follow status changes and read administrator responses in My Reports.

Unlinked reports stay anonymous and cannot be matched to your account later. Account linking is always optional.
