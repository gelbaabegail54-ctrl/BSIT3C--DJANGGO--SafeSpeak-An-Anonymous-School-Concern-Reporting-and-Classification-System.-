How the confidence works

The model tokenizes the text, matches exact words, phrases, and prefix keywords, then weighs the winning category against the rest.

Confidence rises when a category has strong, repeated signals and a clear lead. Reports below {{ low_confidence_threshold }}% are surfaced for review.
