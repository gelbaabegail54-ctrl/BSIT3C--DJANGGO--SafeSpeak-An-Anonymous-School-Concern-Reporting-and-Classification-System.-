Your privacy

You are in control of what you share. Unlinked reports cannot be traced back to your account.

  • Choose whether to link the report before submitting.
  • Avoid images that show faces, IDs, or addresses.

What happens next

  • Submitted — your report enters the review queue.
  • Review — the team checks the details and category.
  • Action — the team responds or refers the concern.
  • Resolved — linked reports show the outcome.
