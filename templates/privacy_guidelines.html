Anonymous by default. Reports and comments are shown as Anonymous. Linking a report to your account is optional and only happens when you choose it before submitting.

How your anonymity works

The choices you make before you press submit.

Protected
  • Unlinked reports are anonymous. They are not connected to your account and cannot be matched to you later, even if you change your mind.
  • Linking is optional. If you tick “Link this report to my account,” the report appears in My Reports so you can track it. Administrators can then see that the report came from your account.
  • Comments are anonymous. Your username is not shown on comments. You can edit or delete your own comments, and administrators can moderate them.
  • Open reports are visible to signed-in users. The home feed shows report text and images to other signed-in members of the community. Do not include identifying details unless they are necessary.
  • Be careful with images. Photos can show faces, uniforms, locations, or personal documents. Only attach images that are needed to explain the report.

What administrators can see

Access stays limited to the people reviewing your report.

Authorized SafeSpeak administrators can review report text, attached images, status, comments, and the account link when you explicitly chose to link a report. They use this information to review reports, respond to reporters, and decide on next steps.

Internal notes and administrative activity logs are used for case management and are not shown in the community feed.

Protecting yourself and others

Small habits that keep the community safe.

  • Do not post passwords, ID numbers, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information.
  • Remove names or identifying details when they are not needed to understand the concern.
  • If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, share only what is necessary and respect their privacy.
  • If a report contains information that should be removed, contact a school administrator.

Community guidelines

What we ask of everyone who speaks up here.

Kindness
  1. Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
  2. Protect privacy. Do not share names, contact details, or other identifying information about yourself or others.
  3. Report honestly. Do not submit false reports or deliberately misleading information.
  4. Keep it relevant. Use comments to discuss the report, not to spam, advertise, or post unrelated content.
  5. Use appropriate images. No explicit, violent, or unrelated images. Attach only what helps explain the concern.

Moderation

Reports and comments that break these guidelines may be dismissed, removed, or referred to the appropriate school office. Misuse of SafeSpeak may also lead to account restrictions.

Reports are reviewed as capacity allows. SafeSpeak is not monitored 24/7 and is not a substitute for emergency services.
